A Bacterial Guanylyl Cyclase Bridges Antiphage Response and Multiple Cellular Processes in Uropathogenic Escherichia coli

About this work

Submitted for the degree of Master of Science in Microbiology at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign.

The work characterises a previously uncharacterised Type-I CBASS antiphage system encoded by uropathogenic Escherichia coli str. KTE100: a guanylyl cyclase sensor and a ClyA-like pore-forming effector, taken from phylogenetic distribution through predicted structure to the system’s effect on the cell.
